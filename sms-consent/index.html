SMS Consent and Opt-In — Jason Tan's Assistant

This page documents the opt-in and consent process for the personal SMS notification service operated by Jason Tan ("Jason Tan's assistant"), sending automated personal event, location, and meetup update text messages from +1 929-307-0166.

Who subscribes

Exactly one subscriber — the brand owner's partner, a known personal contact. There is no public sign-up, no web form, no advertising, and no purchased or third-party leads. No additional recipients will be added without the same process.

How consent is given

Verbally and in person. Before the first message is sent, Jason Tan reads the subscriber the following disclosure verbatim:

"By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS personal event, location, and meetup update notifications from Jason Tan's assistant. Message frequency may vary. Standard Message and Data Rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. We will not share mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes."

The subscriber is also told: the messages will come from 929-307-0166; they are automated updates sent by his assistant during his events (up to about 10 messages on an event day); and consent is not a condition of any purchase. Only after she verbally agrees is her number recorded and a confirmation SMS sent:

"You have agreed to receive SMS updates from Jason Tan's assistant. Msg freq may vary. Std msg & data rates apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

The sole subscriber gave this verbal consent in person before any messages were sent. Her number was provided by her directly to the brand owner; it was not collected through any website, form, advertisement, lead service, or third party.

Opting out and help

Opting out: reply STOP at any time. Help: reply HELP or email liu.jason.tan1@gmail.com. No marketing or promotional content is sent.
