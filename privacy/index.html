Effective November 2, 2026

This policy covers the personal SMS notification service operated by Jason Tan ("Jason Tan's assistant"), which sends automated personal event, location, and meetup update text messages from +1 929-307-0166 to a single subscriber who has consented verbally and in person.

We may share your Personal Data, including your SMS opt-in or consent status, with third parties that help us provide our messaging services, including but not limited to platform providers, phone companies, and any other vendors who assist us in the delivery of text messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency may vary; message and data rates may apply.
